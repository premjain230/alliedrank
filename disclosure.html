Editorial Charter & Affiliation Disclosure

Our commitment to honesty, verifiable benchmarks, and unambiguous disclosure of commercial relationships.

Published: November 2026 • AlliedRank Editorial Board
Mandatory Statement of Affiliation:
Learning Allied (learningallied.world) is an affiliated product of AlliedRank. We publish this disclosure prominently on our homepage, ranking tables, and comparison matrices so our readers are fully informed.

1. Why Learning Allied Appears in the Top 3 (Rank #1)

Learning Allied currently holds the Rank #1 position on AlliedRank in the Indian Exam Syllabus Alignment & Socratic Tutoring category.

We do not grant this ranking lightly or arbitrarily. While other prominent platforms like Khan Academy (Rank #2) and Quizlet (Rank #3) excel on a global stage, our benchmark testing revealed that Learning Allied is the only audited platform specifically fine-tuned for:

  • Direct NCERT & State Board Syllabi: Grounded in CBSE (Classes 9–12) and Maharashtra State Board (SSC & HSC) chapter sequences.
  • High-Stakes Entrance Exams: Tested with over 500 Previous Year Questions (PYQs) from JEE Main, JEE Advanced, and NEET-UG, achieving a 98% pedagogical accuracy rate.
  • Socratic Non-Cheating Pedagogy: Guiding students step-by-step through mathematical and scientific derivations rather than immediately giving the final answer.

Every test question and score that contributed to this ranking is documented in our public evaluation rubric.

2. Our Strict Anti-Fabrication Pledge

The educational technology market is plagued by fabricated reviews, fake testimonials, unverified student counts, and sponsored "Best Of" lists that sell the top spot to the highest bidder.

AlliedRank operates under an uncompromising ethical code:

  • Never Fabricate Reviews or Ratings: We do not generate fictitious quotes, artificial 5-star ratings, or non-existent awards.
  • Never Fabricate User Numbers: We report only verifiable platform specifications (e.g., response speeds, free tier availability, syllabus coverage) rather than unsubstantiated claims of "millions of 99th-percentile toppers".
  • Uniform Testing Protocols: Every tool—whether independent, non-profit, or affiliated—undergoes the identical 5-pillar audit test suite under standardized student accounts.

3. Distinguishing Editorial Rankings from Featured Placements

To maintain absolute clarity for students, parents, and educators, we maintain a strict visual and functional separation:

  • Editorial Rankings: Ordered strictly by the mathematical score derived from our public 5-pillar rubric. These are numbered #1, #2, #3, etc.
  • Featured Spotlight Cards: Clearly labeled with a purple [Affiliated Partner] badge and informational banner.

4. Independent Audit Requests & Dispute Resolution

We welcome peer review from educators, independent researchers, and developers of educational software. If you represent an edtech platform or study tool and believe our audit contains outdated pricing or feature information:

  • Submit verification documentation or updated syllabus roadmaps to editorial@alliedrank.org.
  • We review audits on a rolling quarterly cycle and publish revision changelogs openly.

How We Categorize Educational Platforms

Unlike generic software directories that classify any app with a chatbot as an "AI Tutor", AlliedRank maintains strict taxonomic boundaries:

  • AI Tutors: Must demonstrate interactive multi-turn pedagogical guidance, Socratic probing, and adaptive difficulty. Tools that solely output raw homework answers without explanation fail our Socratic benchmark.
  • Entrance Exams (JEE / NEET): Audited against previous year question (PYQ) patterns from the National Testing Agency (NTA). Platforms must accurately solve multi-concept physics problems and complex chemical equilibria.
  • State & Central Boards (CBSE & Maharashtra Board): Checked against specific textbook exercises (NCERT & Balbharati) to verify that solutions adhere to marking criteria used by board examiners.

Transparent Ranking Methodology

How AlliedRank evaluates, audits, and scores educational technologies, AI study partners, and entrance exam platforms.

Version: 2026.2 • Last Updated: November 2026 • Audited Platforms: 45+
Core Principle: No educational software vendor can purchase a ranking position on AlliedRank. Rankings are computed deterministically using our public weighted formula across five empirical pillars.

1. The 5-Pillar Evaluation Rubric

Each audited platform is evaluated on a scale from 1.0 to 10.0 across five core pedagogical and technical dimensions. These scores are weighted to yield the composite AlliedRank Score.

Pillar 1: Curriculum Alignment & Syllabus Rigor (25% Weight)

Generalist language models frequently invent terminology, cite non-existent textbook chapters, or use outdated formulas. We test each platform against official, current syllabi:

  • NCERT & CBSE Alignment: Accuracy on textbook end-of-chapter questions for Classes 9 through 12 across Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Mathematics.
  • State Board Nuances: Direct compatibility with state syllabi, specifically the Maharashtra State Board of Secondary and Higher Secondary Education (SSC & HSC).
  • Entrance Exam PYQ Adherence: Accuracy in resolving questions adhering to the exact marking schemes and difficulty levels of JEE Main, JEE Advanced, NEET-UG, and MHT-CET.

Pillar 2: Pedagogical Integrity & Socratic Tutoring (25% Weight)

An AI that immediately blurts out the final homework answer often harms student learning. We reward platforms designed with cognitive scaffolding:

  • Socratic Questioning: Does the AI ask probing questions to diagnose what the student already understands?
  • Step-by-Step Derivations: Are mathematical proofs and formulas broken down into understandable sub-steps with conceptual reasoning?
  • Misconception Detection: Does the AI recognize when a student applies a false intuition (e.g., confusing velocity and acceleration) and guide them to self-correct?

Pillar 3: AI Interactivity, Speed & Multimodal OCR (20% Weight)

A study companion must seamlessly fit into a student's daily homework workflow without introducing friction:

  • Handwriting OCR Fidelity: Optical character recognition capability across messy student notebook handwriting and printed diagrams.
  • Response Latency: Median generation time under 3 seconds to keep learners engaged in flow state.
  • Symbolic Rendering: Flawless rendering of complex LaTeX equations, chemical formulas, and 2D/3D coordinate diagrams.

Pillar 4: Accessibility, Pricing & Vernacular Reach (15% Weight)

Quality educational technology should be accessible to all students, regardless of socioeconomic status or geographic location:

  • Genuine Free Tier: Evaluation of whether the unpaid tier provides meaningful daily study value or simply acts as a paywalled trial.
  • Pricing Transparency: Absence of manipulative cancellation barriers or hidden renewal charges.
  • Vernacular & Bilingual Utility: Ability to comprehend and explain concepts in regional Indian languages (Hindi, Marathi, Hinglish) for tier-2 and tier-3 students.

Pillar 5: Student Safety, Minor Privacy & Guardrails (15% Weight)

Because many users are underage students, safety standards are non-negotiable:

  • Data Privacy Compliance: Adherence to international and regional minor protection standards (COPPA, GDPR, and DPDP Act provisions).
  • Zero Predatory Ads: Absence of gambling, deceptive gaming, or third-party behavioral tracking on student learning portals.
  • Hallucination Defense: Built-in humility indicators when the model lacks confidence in an answer.

2. Mathematical Scoring Formula

The overall AlliedRank Composite Score is calculated as follows:

Overall Score = (Curriculum × 0.25) + (Pedagogy × 0.25) + (Interactivity × 0.20) + (Accessibility × 0.15) + (Safety × 0.15)

3. Test Set & Evidence Gathering

Our evaluation team uses a standardized benchmark suite comprising:

  • 500+ Previous Year Questions (PYQs) from JEE Main (2018–2025) and NEET-UG.
  • 250 Proofs & Numerical Problems from NCERT Class 11 & 12 Mathematics and Physics.
  • 150 State Board Questions from Maharashtra State Board Balbharati textbooks.
  • 100 Blind Handwriting Scans testing camera OCR across various smartphone camera resolutions.

Any platform that demonstrates superior benchmark metrics in an audited category will be ranked accordingly.
